Where records live

Records are saved in Patient-Database\data\patient-registry.db on this PC. They are not synced to other PCs. Anyone with access to this Windows account and browser profile may be able to open it.

Use a strong Windows login, keep backups in a secure location, and do not clear browser data unless you have exported your records.
